Fort Salé (Fort Flamand), Salt River Bay

St. Croix, US Virgin Islands · Caribbean and Panama · England 1641; Netherlands 1642; France 1650 · founded 1641 (1641–1650s; NPS park (Columbus Landing NHL))

Evidence: Confirmed Adjacent only

What lies beneath

c. 1,500 years of Saladoid–Taíno occupation; Taíno village and ball court lined with petroglyphs (excavated by Hatt 1923), the only ball court known in the US Virgin Islands; the earthen fort 'sits adjacent to' the village

Relationship

Adjacent only

deliberate reuse of the same headland

Flags

None documented

None documented; some Hatt (1923) artifacts are in the National Museum of Denmark, with no repatriation claim or dispute recorded
