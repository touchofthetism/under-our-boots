Presidio del Norte (Presidio de Nuestra Señora de Belén y Santiago de las Amarillas de la Junta de los Ríos), Ojinaga

Ojinaga, Chihuahua, Mexico (La Junta de los Ríos, confluence of the Río Conchos and Río Bravo) · Mexico and Central America · Spanish Crown (Nueva Vizcaya): built 1759–60 by Capt. Manuel Muñoz after Rubín de Celis; moved to Julimes 1767; re-established at La Junta 1773 by Hugo O Connor; Mexican garrison to 1835 · founded 1760 (1760–1767; 1773–1835)

Evidence: Probable Adjacent only

What lies beneath

The La Junta de los Ríos village cluster: sedentary farming pueblos of the La Junta phase (from c. AD 1200) seen by Cabeza de Vaca in 1535 and later entradas. The 1760 presidio was built beside the native pueblo of Nuestra Señora de Guadalupe, probably within present-day Ojinaga; the presidio site is believed to lie under the modern town and has not been located archaeologically. Related La Junta village sites (Millington, Polvo) are documented on the Texas bank.
