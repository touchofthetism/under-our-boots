Naval Air Weapons Station China Lake (Coso Rock Art NHL; Coso Hot Springs)

California, US · US contiguous West/Southwest · US Navy (Naval Ordnance Test Station) · founded 1943 (1943–present; active)

Evidence: Confirmed Overlaps documented sites

What lies beneath

>1,300 sites inventoried in older counts; the Navy's own page now gives ~2,800 managed sites; station estimated to hold >10,000; Coso Rock Art NHL (36,000 acres; c. 20,000 images in Little Petroglyph Canyon); Coso Hot Springs NRHP district (healing site); Prayer Site

Relationship

Overlaps documented sites

Coso Hot Springs condemned into the base

Flags

Restricted access; Land seizure / displacement; Concealment (alleged)

Restricted access (escorted tours only); Land seizure (Hot Springs condemned 1947); Concealment allegation (geothermal development over an ACHP/SHPO adverse-effect finding; Navy said data 'cannot prove or disprove' harm; NCAI Resolution SAC-12-007, Oct 2012, calls for return to tribes)
