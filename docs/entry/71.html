What lies beneath

Fort Meigs: the National Register lists Fort Meigs Aboriginal (33WO08 and 33WO445, NRIS 98000042, listed 1998) as a prehistoric site under Criterion D with periods of significance AD 1000-1749 at the Fort Meigs State Memorial grounds SW of Perrysburg; a 2002 Toledo Blade report describes human bones quietly moved from a dig site at Fort Meigs.
