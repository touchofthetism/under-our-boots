Centro de Lançamento de Alcântara (CLA)

Maranhão, Brazil · South America · Brazilian Air Force (expropriation decree 12 Sept 1980; activated 1 Mar 1983) · founded 1983 (1983–present; active)

Evidence: Probable Territory taken, no located site

What lies beneath

IPHAN records the Tupinambá village cluster Tapuitapera in the Alcântara area from 1612; no inventory of pre-colonial sites inside the CLA found

Relationship

Territory taken, no located site

historic Tupinambá territory; specific sites unverified

Flags

Land seizure / displacement; Litigation / inquiry; Restricted access

Land seizure/displacement and Litigation (documented — colonial-era quilombola communities, not pre-Columbian): 312 families from 31 settlements moved to agrovilas with no sea access, mainly Jul–Dec 1986 and Nov 1987–Dec 1988 (expropriation by State Decree 7,820 of 12 Sep 1980; CLA created by Federal Decree 88,136 of 1 Mar 1983); IACHR petition 17 Aug 2001, admissibility report 83/06 (2006), merits report 189/20 (2020); Inter-American Court judgment 21 Nov 2024 ordered collective title to 78,105 ha within three years, good-faith consultation and a public act of recognition; Restricted access (launch-window and fishing restrictions on the agrovilas)
