Goose Spit / HMCS Quadra (CFB Comox)

British Columbia, Canada · Canada and Greenland · Britain (Royal Navy) 1896; Canada (RCN 1940; cadet establishment 1956) · founded 1896 (1896–present; active)

Evidence: Confirmed Built directly on a known site

What lies beneath

K'ómoks and Pentlatch burial ground for leaders and medicine on the spit (pélxwikw); by 1876 the Nation was confined to ~13 acres around the burials (IR 3) and 'the rest of the Spit was later taken over by the navy'

Relationship

Built directly on a known site

Flags

Land seizure / displacement; Restricted access

Land seizure (navy takeover beyond the 13-acre reserve); Restricted access (public limited to the beach; 2009 No Spit, No Deal campaign; 2012 Agreement in Principle; 7 May 2019 shared-access agreement)
