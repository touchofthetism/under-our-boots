Pōhakuloa Training Area

Hawaii, US · Hawaii and Alaska · US Army (Camp Pōhakuloa WWII; 1956 EO; 65-year State lease 1964) · founded 1943 (1943–present; active (132,000 acres); State lease expires 16 Aug 2029)

Evidence: Confirmed Overlaps documented sites

What lies beneath

1,198 recorded sites (Sept 2016; 39 eligible, 822 unevaluated); lava-tube habitation caves, shrines, trails, quarries; Bobcat Trail Habitation Cave (NRHP); confirmed iwi kūpuna burials and a reburial crypt; 61,892 acres unsurveyed

Relationship

Overlaps documented sites

no sites in the cantonment; all Traditional Hawaiian sites in training areas

Flags

Destroyed; Restricted access; Litigation / inquiry; Repatriation / NAGPRA

Destroyed (impact area holds MEC, chemical munitions and depleted uranium; petroglyphs shielded during detonations); Restricted access (UXO escort only); Litigation (Ching v. Case: State breached its trust duty — only three inspections in 50 years; Circuit Court 3 Apr 2018; Supreme Court 23 Aug 2019; monitoring plan 20 Apr 2021); Repatriation (two NAGPRA plans of action 2023–24)
