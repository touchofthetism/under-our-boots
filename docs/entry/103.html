Fort Reno

Oklahoma, US · US Plains/Midwest/South-Central · US Army · founded 1875 (1875–1948; USDA Grazinglands Research Laboratory / Bureau of Prisons)

Evidence: Confirmed Territory taken, no located site

What lies beneath

9,493–9,600 acres carved from the Cheyenne–Arapaho reservation by an 1883 Executive Order reserving it exclusively for military purposes; pre-contact site overlap unverified

Relationship

Territory taken, no located site

reservation land taken; no located site

Flags

Land seizure / displacement; Litigation / inquiry; Concealment (alleged)
