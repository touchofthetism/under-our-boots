6º Pelotão Especial de Fronteira (6º PEF) Uiramutã

Uiramutã, Terra Indígena Raposa Serra do Sol, Roraima, Brazil · South America · Brazilian Army (Programa Calha Norte), works from 2000, inaugurated 2 May 2003 · founded 2003 (2003–present)

Evidence: Probable Territory taken, no located site

What lies beneath

Built in the Makuxi aldeia of Uiramutã (converted into a municipal seat in 1995 without Indigenous consent) inside the Raposa Serra do Sol Indigenous Land; Indigenous territory rather than an excavated pre-contact site

Relationship

Territory taken, no located site

barracks placed in a Makuxi village within a demarcated Indigenous land; no archaeological site named

Flags

Land seizure / displacement; Restricted access; Litigation / inquiry

Documented (Baines, FUNAI Revista Estudos e Pesquisas): pelotão implanted from 2000 'sem consultar as lideranças indígenas'; the Army inaugurated the quartel on 2 May 2003 ignoring a pending lawsuit by the Indigenous communities against its construction; 'Operação Uiramutã' staged to coincide with Indigenous celebrations with shows of force and acts of intimidation (as described by the author).
