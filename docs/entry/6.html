Joint Base Lewis-McChord (Fort Lewis / Camp Lewis; McChord Field)

Washington, US · US contiguous West/Southwest · US Army 1917; USAAC 1938; merged 2010 · founded 1917 (1917–present; active)

Evidence: Confirmed Built directly on a known site

What lies beneath

Nisqually homeland and 1854 Medicine Creek Treaty reservation: 3,353 acres (about two-thirds of the reservation) condemned 1917–18; ~473 recorded sites (JBLM; an earlier handout said 375) up to 8,000 years old incl. two main villages (Yil-whaltz), an ancient burial ground and family cemeteries

Relationship

Built directly on a known site

Flags

Land seizure / displacement; Litigation / inquiry

Land seizure (condemnation 4 Feb 1918; ruling 4 Apr 1918; $75,840; two weeks to leave; $65,000 compensation 1921); Litigation (Nisqually challenge lost); shells fell short over the reservation in the 1970s; 1986 MOA, 1991 hatchery lease, 2008 canoe landing
