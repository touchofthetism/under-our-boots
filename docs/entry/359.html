Fuerte Bulnes (Punta Santa Ana)

Magallanes, Chile · South America · Chile 1843 · founded 1843 (1843–1840s; site museum)

Evidence: Confirmed Overlaps documented sites

What lies beneath

Punta Santa Ana holds a sequence of marine hunter-gatherer (canoe-people, ancestral Kawesqar) shell-midden sites: PSA-1 (c. 6300 BP, Ortiz-Troncoso), PSA-2 (c. 2700 BP) and PSA-3 (c. 700-1000 BP), with PSA-3's upper layers disturbed by the mid-20th-century reconstruction of Fuerte Bulnes and vehicle traffic (Morello et al., Magallania 40(2), 2012)
