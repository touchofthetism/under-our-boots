BDF Conservation Post at Caracol (Belize Defence Force Caracol camp, Chiquibul)

Caracol Archaeological Reserve, Cayo District, Belize (near the Guatemalan border) · Mexico and Central America · Government of Belize / Belize Defence Force with the Institute of Archaeology (NICH); inaugurated July 2015 · founded 2015 (2015–present)

Evidence: Probable Deliberate reuse of an Indigenous place

What lies beneath

Caracol, the largest Classic Maya city in Belize (c.600 BC–AD 900, with over 25,000 structures across c.177 km2). The permanent BDF post, housing twelve soldiers with quarters, showers and radio, was placed inside the Caracol Archaeological Reserve near the site core so that soldiers can patrol the epicentre and surroundings after the 2014 killing of a tourism police officer at the ruins; it is linked to the Douglas D Silva and Belizario Camp bases.
