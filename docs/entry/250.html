Venado Beach (Playa Venado) cemetery in the Fort Kobbe target range

Former Canal Zone, Panama · Caribbean and Panama · US Army (Fort Kobbe); US Navy 15th Naval District · founded 1930 (1930s–1999; returned 1999)

Evidence: Confirmed Built directly on a known site

What lies beneath

Gran Coclé cemetery (c. AD 200–900): 369 individuals recorded, ~20% abnormal burials, ~25% with mutilation (trophy heads, decapitation); gold and tumbaga frogs and nose rings

Relationship

Built directly on a known site

cemetery inside the target-shooting area

Flags

Destroyed; Concealment (documented)

Destroyed and Concealment of context (documented): a Navy bulldozer stripping topsoil exposed the graves in 1948; soldiers did 'a great deal of unrecorded digging' before 1951; Lothrop excavated >200 burials, amateur collectors >150, a colonel 19; pieces and the field notebook sold to the Museum of Primitive Art in 1971 (now at the Met; related pieces at the Art Institute of Chicago and Dumbarton Oaks); no Panamanian claim found; ACP inventory calls the site 'heavily disturbed'
