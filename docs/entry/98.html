Badlands Bombing Range (Pine Ridge Gunnery Range) / Ellsworth AFB retained area

South Dakota, US · US Plains/Midwest/South-Central · US Army Air Forces 1942; SD National Guard; USAF · founded 1942 (1942–1968; mostly returned 1968 (USAF retains 2,486 acres))

Evidence: Confirmed Deliberate reuse of an Indigenous place

What lies beneath

341,726 acres of the Pine Ridge Reservation taken by eminent domain in 1942, incl. the Stronghold Table of 1890 Ghost Dance significance

Relationship

Flags

Land seizure / displacement; Destroyed; Restricted access
