Campo de entrenamiento militar Pampa Campanani (also spelled Campapane; Socoroma; Ejército de Chile artillery and mortar training ground)

Pampa Campanani, Socoroma, comuna de Putre, Región de Arica y Parinacota, Chile · South America · Chilean Army; fiscal land assigned by Ministerio de Bienes Nacionales (Decreto Exento N° 215 of 5 July 2017) for artillery and mortar training · founded 2017 (2017–suspended 2018)

Evidence: Probable Overlaps documented sites

What lies beneath

Archaeological study cited by the Corte Suprema: inside Pampa Campanani (southern edge of the middle Lluta valley) a rock shelter ('alerón') with human occupation attributable to hunters and herders, 'caminos troperos' forming part of a prehispanic and posthispanic regional traffic route, plus post-Hispanic mining camp ruins; the Consejo de Monumentos Nacionales stated that more archaeological evidence would be recorded as the land is surveyed

Relationship

Overlaps documented sites

recorded pre-Hispanic sites inside the training ground; Aymara community territory

Flags

Destroyed; Litigation / inquiry

Documented litigation: Comunidad Indígena Aymara Chucuruma filed a recurso de protección against the Ministries of Bienes Nacionales and Defensa; the Corte Suprema (Tercera Sala, July 2018, divided ruling) suspended the effects of the land-assignment decree until measures to protect the archaeological remains found and still to be found are determined and implemented, holding that artillery/mortar training would breach the State's duty of protection. Destruction was threatened, not documented as occurred. Putre mayor Maricel Gutiérrez asked Bienes Nacionales to annul the decree and relocate the exercises, citing other military-related damage in the region (vehicle driven over the Lluta wetland) and asking that Indigenous use and rights over the land be recognised.
