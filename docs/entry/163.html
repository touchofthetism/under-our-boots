Fort Niagara

New York, US · US East/Southeast · France 1726; Britain 1759; US Army 1796–1963 · founded 1726 (1726–1963; state historic site)

Evidence: Confirmed Built directly on a known site

What lies beneath

Fort Niagara NHL nomination additional documentation: excavations inside the fort walls since 1979 record stratified deposits documenting more than 10,000 years of occupation, with Late Woodland aboriginal ceramics and lithics in the lowermost sublayer D4 beneath European material and incised Late Woodland Iroquoian ceramics in a hearth; Paleo-Indian through Late Woodland stages (c. 8000 BC-AD 1600) tabulated; Seneca permission for the 1720 trading post
