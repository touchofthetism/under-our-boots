Fort Zoutman

Aruba (Netherlands) · Caribbean and Panama · Netherlands (WIC) · founded 1798 (1798–; museum)

Evidence: Probable Adjacent only

What lies beneath

No find on the fort footprint; the National Archaeological Museum Aruba 2013 report on Plaza Daniel Leo in Old Oranjestad records Caquetío Ceramic Period (c. 900/1000–1515) ceramics and turtle bone mixed with colonial material a few streets from Fort Zoutman, and Hartog records Amerindian urns and possible human bones found in 1875 at the Paardenbaai battery site Forti Abao; built with Amerindian corvée-supplied materials
