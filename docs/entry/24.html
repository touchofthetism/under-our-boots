Naval Air Station Fallon / Fallon Range Training Complex

Nevada, US · US contiguous West/Southwest · US Navy (Army airfield 1942) · founded 1942 (1942–present; active (expanded ~600,000 acres, FY2021 NDAA))

Episode 3 — NAS Fallon — publishedEvidence: Confirmed Overlaps documented sites

What lies beneath

1,069 sites (June 2016), 113 NRHP-eligible; Navy study found 900 places of cultural/religious importance to Fallon Paiute-Shoshone, Walker River Paiute and Yomba Shoshone, about half under range airspace

Relationship

Overlaps documented sites

Flags

Destroyed; Restricted access; Concealment (documented)

Destroyed and Concealment-by-process (documented): under the 2011 PA 'all of the existing B-20 range is exempt from further Section 106 review' because explosives already disturbed it; a TCP in B-20 was judged to have lost the trait that made it special; Restricted access (~90 miles of fencing; tribal objections to access limits and lack of survey)
