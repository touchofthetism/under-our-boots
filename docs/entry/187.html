Mākua Military Reservation

Hawaii, US · Hawaii and Alaska · US Army · founded 1920 (1920s–2026 (whole Mākua–Kaʻena area taken 7 Dec 1941); live fire halted 2004, ended permanently (announced Dec 2023); ranges closed 1 Oct 2026 and close-out of the State lease (782 acres) requested, per Hawaii News Now 6 Nov 2026)

Evidence: Confirmed Deliberate reuse of an Indigenous place

What lies beneath

Mākua and Kahanahāiki valleys, 'an important site in legends about the origin of the Hawaiian people'; Ukanipō heiau (NRHP 1982); Kāneana cave; 117 sites in the ICRMP (12 eligible); Earthjustice: >100 cultural sites incl. heiau, ahu, burials and petroglyphs

Relationship

Deliberate reuse of an Indigenous place

sacred valley; overlaps documented sites in the impact area

Flags

Destroyed; Restricted access; Land seizure / displacement; Litigation / inquiry

Destroyed (live fire; 22 Jul 2003 escaped burn of 2,100 acres exposed unrecorded terraces); Restricted access (access cut from 12+ sites to one on 27 Feb 2005, three reopened 2008); Land seizure (1941); Litigation (Mālama Mākua v. Rumsfeld: 2001 injunction and settlement requiring an EIS and cultural access; Army 'never completed the EIS'; back in court 2008 and 2016; 2025 ROD relinquishes the 782 State acres)
