Carlisle Barracks (Carlisle Indian Industrial School cemetery)

Pennsylvania, US · US East/Southeast · Britain/US Army · founded 1757 (1757–present; active (Army War College))

Evidence: Probable Built directly on a known site

What lies beneath

Post-contact: cemetery of the Indian Industrial School (1879–1918); 180 of 229 plots believed to hold Native children; Army moved the cemetery in 1927 (22 identities lost); disinterments since 2017: 58 students returned through 2025 (5 in 2023; 11 in 2024; 19 in 2025), ninth round of 12 in 2026; 138 graves remain; national monument 9 Dec 2024

Relationship

Built directly on a known site

post-contact Indigenous burial ground, not a pre-colonial site

Flags

Repatriation / NAGPRA; Restricted access

Repatriation (ongoing Army-funded disinterments; 'more than 160 are still waiting'); Restricted access (cemetery closed with privacy fencing during work)
