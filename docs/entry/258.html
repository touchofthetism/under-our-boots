CFB Goose Bay / 5 Wing Goose Bay

Newfoundland and Labrador, Canada · Canada and Greenland · Canada (RCAF) with US and UK; NATO low-level training 1981–2005 · founded 1941 (1941–present; active)

Evidence: Confirmed Territory taken, no located site

What lies beneath

Innu Nitassinan (ancestral homeland); no Innu or Inuit archaeological site on the base documented

Relationship

Territory taken, no located site

dispossession confirmed; sites unverified

Flags

Land seizure / displacement; Litigation / inquiry; Destroyed

Land seizure (use without consent; low-level flying began 'without any prior consultation'; ~7,000 flights/yr by 1990); Litigation/inquiry (~200 Innu arrests; April 1989 Igloliorte acquittal overturned; 1990 convictions; 1990 panel found the EIS inadequate); Destroyed (contamination at >100 sites; $142.9M remediation to 2022)
