Chaco War fortines (Isla Po'i, Pilcomayo fortines, Ingavi, Boquerón)

Chaco, Paraguay/Bolivia · South America · Bolivian Army and Paraguayan Army · founded 1905 (c. 1905–1935; battlefield monuments)

Evidence: Confirmed Deliberate reuse of an Indigenous place

What lies beneath

Bolivia placed most fortines near Nivaclé villages; Isla Po'i 'was built in the middle of Enlhet territory'; Ingavi in Ayoreo territory — living Indigenous villages of the early 20th century, not archaeological sites

Relationship

Deliberate reuse of an Indigenous place

living villages (20th c.)

Flags

Land seizure / displacement; Destroyed

Land seizure/displacement and Destroyed (massacres; an aerial bombing wiped out a Nivaclé village; Enlhet forced to guide, build roads and dig trenches; after 1935 the Nivaclé were disarmed and ranchers took their land)
