Cuartel Bellavista (Museo Nacional de Costa Rica)

San José, Costa Rica · Mexico and Central America · Costa Rican Army 1915–30 · founded 1917 (1917–1948; museum since 1950)

Evidence: Refuted No site

What lies beneath

No pre-Columbian remains on the hill; the only excavation (2009) exposed a military tunnel with buttons, casings and bullets

Relationship

No site

Concealment (alleged)

Concealment allegation (post-contact): the military sealed the tunnels before the 1950 handover; unverified stories of people killed or hidden after 1948
