Fort Snelling (Historic Fort Snelling at Bdote)

Minnesota, US · US Plains/Midwest/South-Central · US Army · founded 1819 (1819–1946; MNHS historic site / state park / national cemetery)

Evidence: Confirmed Deliberate reuse of an Indigenous place

What lies beneath

Bdote, the Minnesota–Mississippi confluence: at least 10,000 years of gathering; Dakota place of creation; named sacred sites Taku Wakaŋ Tipi, Mni Owe Sni (Coldwater Spring), Oheyawahi (Pilot Knob); Pike Island

Relationship

Deliberate reuse of an Indigenous place

Flags

Land seizure / displacement; Concealment (alleged)

Land seizure (1805 Pike treaty, payment left blank, ~155,320 acres); displacement (Nov 1862–May 1863 internment of 1,658 Dakota non-combatants below the fort; 102–300 deaths; exile to Crow Creek); Concealment allegation (April 2019 Senate committee voted to cut $4M from MNHS over signs adding the Dakota name Bdote; MNHS removed the signs; name retained 23 Jun 2022; Dakota descendant: 'They caved')
