Forte de Santo Antônio de Gurupá (Dutch Fort Mariocai, 1601–1619; Portuguese fort from 1623)

Gurupá, Pará (lower Amazon / Xingu mouth), Brazil · South America · Dutch (1601–1619); Portuguese under Pedro Teixeira and Bento Maciel Parente from 1623; Brazilian Army (8ª Região Militar) small garrison still in 1958; passed to the Ministry of Defence in 1963; restored 2014–2018 · founded 1601 (1601/1623–20th century (garrison); museum since 2018)

Evidence: Probable Built directly on a known site

What lies beneath

Museu Paraense Emílio Goeldi (projeto OCA) research classifies the fort locale as 'um complexo arqueológico indígena pré-colonial e pós-colonial'; the fort was 'construída em 1623 por portugueses sobre uma grande aldeia indígena' (DOL/Goeldi complaint, 2020); restoration 2014–2018 ran concurrently with archaeological excavation whose results are displayed in the fort museum

Relationship

Built directly on a known site

Portuguese fort built over a large Indigenous village; site classed as a pre-colonial Indigenous archaeological complex

Destroyed

Documented: fort built on top of a large Indigenous village (1623); in Sept 2020 the on-site museum/exhibition was vandalised and looted after being left without surveillance, prompting a Museu Goeldi complaint to the MPF and Iphan. No Indigenous litigation recorded.
