Naval Weapons Station Yorktown (Navy Mine Depot; Kiskiak)

Virginia, US · US East/Southeast · US Navy · founded 1918 (1918–present; active)

Evidence: Confirmed Built directly on a known site

What lies beneath

Powhatan-affiliated town of Kiskiack/Chiskiack: palisade, ditch, pottery, pipes; 'the heart of the Kiskiak Indian Tribe's village' sits on the bluff under 1920 officers' housing; NAVFAC calls the 13,000-acre station an unparalleled East Coast archaeological park

Relationship

Built directly on a known site

Flags

Restricted access; Land seizure / displacement

Restricted access (base closed for ~100 years; <1% of village excavated; artifacts Navy property); Land seizure (Kiskiack driven west before 1627 after the 1622–23 wars)
