Fort Hood (Fort Cavazos 2023–2025)

Texas, US · US Plains/Midwest/South-Central · US Army · founded 1942 (1942–present; active)

Evidence: Confirmed Overlaps documented sites

What lies beneath

>2,000 prehistoric and historic sites; camps to ~10,000 years; Cowhouse Creek rock shelters; at least four sites with human remains; seven tribes consulted

Relationship

Overlaps documented sites

Flags

Destroyed; Repatriation / NAGPRA

Destroyed (looting: 1985 recovery of 48 individuals from a vandalized burial shelter; 2016 felony conviction); Repatriation (22 Nov 1991 reburial by the Comanche cemetery committee; Oct 2018 transfer to the Tonkawa Tribe; Notice of Inventory Completion 9 Oct 2024)
