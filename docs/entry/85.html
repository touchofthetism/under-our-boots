Fort Scott

Kansas, US · US Plains/Midwest/South-Central · US Army · founded 1842 (1842–1853; 1861–65; NPS site)

Evidence: Probable Overlaps documented sites

What lies beneath

Permanent Indian Frontier post on land acquired from the Cherokee Neutral Lands; NPS Midwest Archeological Center investigations (Clark 1993, Technical Report 28) recovered chert flakes in several areas of the fort and the park museum holds an Early Woodland contracting-stemmed projectile point found behind HS-6 and a chipped-stone hoe said to come from the fort; Clark judges these suggestive but not conclusive of a prehistoric component
