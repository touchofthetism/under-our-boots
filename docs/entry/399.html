Forte de Óbidos (Forte Pauxis, 1697; 19th-century Fortaleza de Óbidos; Quartel General Gurjão, 1907)

Óbidos, Pará (narrowest point of the Amazon), Brazil · South America · Portuguese colonial government (Forte Pauxis started 1697 under Manoel da Mota Siqueira / Antônio de Albuquerque Coelho de Carvalho, with Indigenous labour); Brazilian Empire/Republic rebuilt a fort in the 19th century and a Quartel General (Gurjão) inaugurated 1907 · founded 1697 (1697–20th century (Quartel Gurjão reported abandoned by 1982))

Evidence: Probable Built directly on a known site

What lies beneath

Excavations by Lucena (1994, 1996) in the parapet of the Forte de Óbidos found evidence of pre-colonial occupation in every cut: 'A superfície em que se deu início à instalação do Forte representava o topo de uma sucessão de camadas de ocupação indígena', with terra preta de índio across all excavated areas of the fort and the Indigenous occupation extending well beyond its footprint; the 1697 fort was named for the Pauxis people settled around the military post
