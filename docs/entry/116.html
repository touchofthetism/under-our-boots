Marine Corps Recruit Depot Parris Island (Santa Elena / Charlesfort / Fort San Marcos)

South Carolina, US · US East/Southeast · France 1562; Spain 1566–87; US Navy 1891; USMC 1915 · founded 1562 (1562–63; 1566–87; 1891–present; active)

Evidence: Probable Overlaps documented sites

What lies beneath

Orista and Escamacu settlements around Santa Elena; island occupied >4,000 years; ~100 archaeological sites on the depot (3 NRHP-listed); Santa Elena/Charlesfort NHL beneath a former military golf course; Fort San Marcos (1577) located 2016

Restricted access
