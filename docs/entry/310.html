Pituffik Space Base (Thule Air Base; Dundas / Uummannaq)

Greenland (Denmark) · Canada and Greenland · United States (1951 Denmark–US Defence Agreement) · founded 1951 (1951–present; active)

Evidence: Confirmed Built directly on a known site

What lies beneath

Inughuit village Uummannaq (Danish 'Thule'/Dundas) and its Dundas Peninsula hunting grounds; Comer's Midden (1916), the proto-Inuit village that gave the Thule culture its name; the 1953 expansion evicted the village (houses, hospital, school, church, graveyard left behind)

Relationship

Built directly on a known site

Flags

Land seizure / displacement; Litigation / inquiry; Restricted access

Land seizure/displacement (told 25 May 1953; 26 families/116 people moved >100 km to Qaanaaq within days); Litigation (Eastern High Court 20 Aug 1999: expropriation, DKK 500,000 to the tribe plus DKK 25,000/15,000 per person; Supreme Court 28 Nov 2003 upheld; ECHR 12 Jan 2006 inadmissible ratione temporis); Restricted access (defence area); Concealment (documented): the Prime Minister's Office admitted the relocation was 'a serious interference and unlawful conduct' (apology 2 Sept 1999); separately the 21 Jan 1968 B-52 crash and 40-year contamination controversy
