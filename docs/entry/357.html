Puerto Williams (Navy town and base, Isla Navarino)

Magallanes, Chile · South America · Chilean Navy (founded 15 Nov 1953) · founded 1953 (1953–present; active)

Evidence: Confirmed Built directly on a known site

What lies beneath

Yagán territory occupied >6,000 years; dated occupations beside the town (Bahía Virginia, Caleta Róbalo, Ensenada Villarino, Río Róbalo); Villa Ukika shell midden (c. 1200–270 BP); lithic site PW-70 on the waterfront; 2017–18 rescue excavations (results unpublished)
