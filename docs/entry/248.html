US Naval Operating Base Chaguaramas

Trinidad and Tobago · Caribbean and Panama · US Navy (Destroyers-for-Bases lease) · founded 1941 (1941–1967; returned)

Evidence: Probable Adjacent only

What lies beneath

No pre-contact site is published on the Chaguaramas peninsula itself; Boomert (2016, The Indigenous Peoples of Trinidad and Tobago) records a Saladoid habitation site at Sanders Bay on Chacachacare Island and a Saladoid effigy jar (c. AD 350–650/800) recovered underwater in 1990 at Bombshell Bay off Gaspar Grande, both in the Bocas islands flanking the former US base

Adjacent only

Flags

Land seizure / displacement; Restricted access

Land seizure/displacement (1941 resettlement of peninsula residents; homes demolished by 1942); Restricted access (peninsula closed 1943–44; Eric Williams's 1960 March in the Rain)
