Ramey Air Force Base (Borinquen Field)

Aguadilla, Puerto Rico (US) · Caribbean and Panama · US Army Air Corps / USAF · founded 1939 (1939–1973; closed (returned by 1978))

Evidence: Confirmed Overlaps documented sites

What lies beneath

Borinquen site (Rouse 1937; shell deposit with Cuevas, Ostiones and Capá ceramics); only four pre-Columbian sites recorded in all Aguadilla, partly because the base took an extensive area

Relationship

Overlaps documented sites

probable undocumented destruction across the footprint

Flags

Land seizure / displacement; Destroyed; Concealment (documented)

Land seizure (eviction of Maleza Alta and Maleza Baja communities, 1939–40s); Destroyed and Concealment-by-omission (documented by the PR SHPO): 1940s construction personnel 'were military and had neither the knowledge nor the interest' to record finds; sites possibly impacted, displaced or destroyed without record; later reuse avoided new surveys
