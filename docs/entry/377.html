Centre Spatial Guyanais (Kourou–Sinnamary)

French Guiana (France) · South America · France (CNES) — civil spaceport · founded 1967 (1967–present; active)

Evidence: Confirmed Built directly on a known site

What lies beneath

Inrap excavation Eva 2 (2006): an Archaic occupation c. 4500–2000 BC in a palaeosol, ceramics from c. 2200 BC; 'la carrière dite Eva' was quarried by the CSG for the Soyuz launch pad; Luna diagnostic (2015) for Ariane 6 found Archaic and historic Amerindian occupations
