Sistema de fuertes de Valdivia (Mancera, Corral, Niebla, Amargos)

Los Ríos, Chile · South America · Spain 1645–47 · founded 1645 (1645–1820; monuments)

Evidence: Probable Overlaps documented sites

What lies beneath

Huilliche territory. Test pits by Rodrigo Mera, Leonor Adan, Simon Urbina and others (Fondart project, 5-9 Nov 2007) in the Casa del Castellano and beside the church inside Castillo San Pedro de Alcantara on Isla Mancera reached cultural deposits to c.1.5 m with 'restos culturales prehispanicos' on the same terrace on which the castle was built: Vergel and Valdivia-phase ceramics and possibly Pitren pottery beneath colonial and republican layers. No pre-Hispanic deposit is published for Niebla, Corral or Amargos
