Morro de Arica (1880 Peruvian forts; Chilean monuments and Museo Histórico y de Armas)

Arica, Chile · South America · Peru 1880; Chilean Army (museum 1974) · founded 1880 (1880–present; monument and museum)

Evidence: Confirmed Overlaps documented sites

What lies beneath

Northern slope used by the Chinchorro culture for >5,000 years: Morro 1 (largest excavated Chinchorro cemetery on the coastal cordillera, 105 individuals, 5160–3670 BP), Morro 1/5, Morro 1/6, Mirador La Virgen; Faldeo Norte del Morro de Arica is a component of the UNESCO Chinchorro property (2021)
