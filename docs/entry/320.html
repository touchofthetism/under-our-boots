Ciudadela de San Benito (Convento de San Francisco / Cuartel de San Benito), Mérida

Yucatán, Mexico · Mexico and Central America · Spain (convent 1537; citadel 1669); Mexican state/federal forces (cuartel and jail 19th c.) · founded 1669 (1669–mid-19th c.; demolished (site now Mercado de San Benito))

Evidence: Confirmed Built directly on a known site

What lies beneath

Maya city of T'Hó: the 'cerro de San Benito' was an artificial Maya temple platform of >20,000 m²; the 1537 convent was built with stone quarried from the Maya temples on it

Relationship

Built directly on a known site

Flags

Destroyed; Land seizure / displacement

Destroyed (Maya temple stones quarried 1537; demolition from 1861, land ceded to a railway company; 'the last Maya vestiges were destroyed in the early 20th century'; citadel gone 1973); Land seizure
