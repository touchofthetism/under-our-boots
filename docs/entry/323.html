Base Aérea Militar No. 1 Santa Lucía / Aeropuerto Internacional Felipe Ángeles (AIFA)

Zumpango, State of Mexico, Mexico · Mexico and Central America · Mexican Air Force; SEDENA (airport built by Army engineers 2019–22) · founded 2019 (mid-20th c.–present; active)

Evidence: Confirmed Overlaps documented sites

What lies beneath

Lake Xaltocan shore: INAH salvage (May 2019–May 2022) studied 112 skeletons from 104 burials — Coyotlatelco-phase occupation (AD 600–900; Tlatel 7, Sin Cuadrícula 5) and a Late Postclassic burial group of 58 burials/66 individuals at the Xaltocan site; >300 mammoths and >20,000 bones

Relationship

Overlaps documented sites

discovered during military construction

Flags

Concealment (documented); Restricted access; Litigation / inquiry

Concealment (documented): the Aug 2019 INAH–SEDENA agreement's Clause 16 declared information on the finds reserved under the Transparency Law as national security/defence, revealed by a freedom-of-information request on 13 Jan 2020, contradicting the president's Oct 2019 pledge; Restricted access; Litigation (amparo; 17 Jun 2020 judicial order to protect the excavation area; 2 Jun 2020 PRI transparency demand in Congress)
