Joint Base Pearl Harbor–Hickam (Pearl Harbor Naval Base; Hickam Field; Fort Kamehameha; Ford Island; Iroquois Point; McGrew Point)

Hawaii, US · Hawaii and Alaska · US Navy 1908; US Army 1907/1935; USAF · founded 1908 (1908–present; active)

Evidence: Confirmed Overlaps documented sites

What lies beneath

Puʻuloa, home of the shark goddess Kaʻahupāhau; 22 fishponds historically, only three 'relatively intact' (Loko Kunana and Loko Muliwai filled); Loko Paʻaiau (AD 1327–1473) under Navy restoration; Ford Island (Mokuʻumeʻume, Makahiki island) enlarged with fill; sand-dune burials at Fort Kamehameha and Iroquois Point

Relationship

Overlaps documented sites

Fort Kamehameha housing over sand burials; fishponds filled

Flags

Destroyed; Repatriation / NAGPRA

Destroyed (fishponds filled; burials disturbed by 1940 Hickam construction, quarters and parking lots); Repatriation (FR 20 Jun 1997: 9 individuals removed 1940–89; FR 23 Feb 1999: 3 from Iroquois Point; Halealoha Haleamau burial vault holds 98 iwi; protective wall 14 Jul 2025)
