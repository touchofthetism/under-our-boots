Base militar del Cerro El Alguacil (Inarwa) — Batallón de Artillería N° 2 'La Popa' / Décima Brigada Blindada communications base

Cerro El Alguacil, Pueblo Bello–Valledupar, Cesar (Sierra Nevada de Santa Marta), Colombia · South America · Colombian Army (Ministerio de Guerra), from 1962; hill deeded to the Ministry by Escritura Pública 104 of 25 Feb 1965; antennas of Policía, RTVC, Telecom added 1980s–1990s · founded 1962 (1962–present)

Evidence: Confirmed Built directly on a known site

What lies beneath

Inarwa (Inarwa Tama), a sacred hill of the Arhuaco (Iku) people, 'padre de los alimentos', site of pagamento offerings; Incoder certifies it lies inside the Resguardo Arhuaco and the Línea Negra; the Ministry of Defence disputes this. Sacred natural site, not an excavated archaeological site.

Relationship

Built directly on a known site

military base and >480 antennas on a named Arhuaco sacred hill; sacred geography rather than excavated remains

Flags

Land seizure / displacement; Restricted access; Litigation / inquiry

Documented: Confederación Indígena Tayrona states the Arhuaco were barred from rituals on the hill from 1962 to 2004 and since then need permits; Army guard post and control barrier; complaint cites anti-personnel mines around the hill. Litigation: Corte Constitucional T-005 of 2016 ordered free access for pagamento ceremonies and a consulta previa on the cultural impact of the base and antennas (Consejo de Estado had revoked the lower court's consulta order); the complaint's request to remove base/antennas was not granted in the visible text.
