Dutch Harbor Naval Operating Base and Fort Mears (Amaknak Island)

Alaska, US · Hawaii and Alaska · US Navy 1911/1940; US Army 1940–44 · founded 1940 (1940–1945; closed (NHL; Ounalashka Corporation))

Evidence: Unverified Overlaps documented sites

What lies beneath

Fort Mears on the island's only flat terrain, the Margaret Bay site area; Amaknak Bridge site (2,800–3,300 BP; stone-walled houses) excavated 2003

Margaret Bay site

Land seizure / displacement

Land seizure/displacement (~900 Unangax̂ evacuated to Southeast Alaska camps 1942–45); Destroyed (returning villagers found homes and churches 'damaged or destroyed, mainly by the U.S. military')
