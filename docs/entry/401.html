Escuela de Fuerzas Especiales de Infantería de Marina (Armada Nacional), Barrancón

Barrancón, San José del Guaviare, Guaviare, Colombia · South America · Colombian Navy / Ministerio de Defensa; land ('Agua Bonita') awarded to the Ministry by Incora in May 1998, after the Resguardo Jiw de Barrancón had been constituted · founded 1990 (late 1990s–present (relocation ordered 2012))

Evidence: Probable Territory taken, no located site

What lies beneath

Resguardo Jiw (Guayabero) de Barrancón: the Corte Constitucional found 19 constructions of the naval school (7 permanent: pool, observation tower, lodging, water tank and plant, school, classrooms, two commando tracks) on 6 ha superimposed on the resguardo; Indigenous territory, no excavated pre-contact site cited

Relationship

Territory taken, no located site

naval special-forces school overlapping a titled Jiw resguardo; the Court called it an 'invasión' and 'superposición'

Flags

Land seizure / displacement; Restricted access; Litigation / inquiry

Documented: Corte Constitucional (Auto of 3 Aug 2012, follow-up to T-025/2004 on Jiw and Nükak peoples) ordered the Ministry of Defence to return the land and relocate the base within six months, accompanied by Defensoría del Pueblo, Pastoral Social and ACNUR; press reported three Indigenous people injured by unexploded munitions found near the school; the Defence Minister said the government would appeal.
