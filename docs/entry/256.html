Cold Lake (Primrose Lake) Air Weapons Range / CFB Cold Lake (4 Wing)

Alberta–Saskatchewan, Canada · Canada and Greenland · Canada (RCAF) · founded 1954 (1954–present; active (11,700 km² range))

Evidence: Confirmed Territory taken, no located site

What lies beneath

Dënesųłiné (Cold Lake First Nations, Treaty 6) and Cree (Canoe Lake, Treaty 10) hunting, trapping and wintering territory around Primrose Lake; stone tools and pottery >5,000 years around Cold Lake; no site inventory for the range found

Relationship

Territory taken, no located site

dispossession confirmed; sites inside the range not documented

Flags

Land seizure / displacement; Litigation / inquiry; Restricted access

Land seizure (1953–54: 11,630 km² taken 'abruptly and without prior notice'; cabins and possessions abandoned; people never allowed back); Litigation/inquiry (Indian Claims Commission's first inquiry 1992–93 found Canada broke Treaties 6 and 10; Canoe Lake $12M settlement; Cold Lake settlement 2002 with land, compensation and range access); Restricted access (traditional use now under agreed arrangements with DND)
