Barry M. Goldwater Range (East: USAF/Luke AFB; West: MCAS Yuma)

Arizona, US · US contiguous West/Southwest · USAAF/USAF and USMC · founded 1941 (1941–present; active)

Evidence: Confirmed Overlaps documented sites

What lies beneath

1,269 sites (c. 18% of BMGR East surveyed); 150 sites in impact zones; a geoglyph tribes consider sacred found by an EOD team; sacred trails; 23 tribes consulted

Relationship

Overlaps documented sites

Flags
