Eskimonæs / Daneborg (Sirius Patrol headquarters)

Greenland (Denmark) · Canada and Greenland · Denmark 1931/1941; US (Daneborg 1944) · founded 1941 (1941–present (Daneborg active))

Evidence: Probable Built directly on a known site

What lies beneath

Eskimonæs was built at the abandoned settlement of the last Northeast Greenland Inuit (four houses, two excavated by Koch's 1929–30 expedition; Clavering met twelve Inuit there in 1823)
