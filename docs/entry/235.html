La Navidad (Columbus's first fort) at En Bas Saline

Haiti · Caribbean and Panama · Spain (Columbus) · founded 1492 (Dec 1492–1493; archaeological site under a village)

Evidence: Confirmed Built directly on a known site

What lies beneath

En Bas Saline (~95,000 m²), one of the largest Taíno towns in Haiti and the principal town of cacique Guacanagarí; the burned central structure yielded the European finds; 'the most likely candidate for La Navidad'
