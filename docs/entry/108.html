Fort Bliss (Texas and New Mexico: McGregor, Doña Ana, Otero Mesa ranges)

Texas/New Mexico, US · US Plains/Midwest/South-Central · US Army · founded 1848 (1848–present; active (~1.12 million acres))

Evidence: Confirmed Overlaps documented sites

What lies beneath

>20,600 archaeological sites (>19,000 and >2,500 NRHP-eligible in FY2010; >85% inventoried); state SHPOs: >98% of known sites predate the Army; Paleoindian camps; Jornada Mogollon pueblos (Madera Quemada); Hueco Bolson; Otero Mesa petroglyphs; seven tribes consult

Restricted access; Destroyed; Repatriation / NAGPRA
