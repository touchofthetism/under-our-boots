Cuartel BIM 'Los Cabitos' N° 51 (Ayacucho)

Ayacucho, Peru · South America · Peruvian Army · founded 1950 (20th c.–present; active (La Hoyada memorial zone))

Evidence: Unverified Unclassified / unknown

What lies beneath

No pre-Hispanic site is documented under the barracks. Perez Calderon and Paredes (Arqueologia y Sociedad 32, 2016) list 'la Hoyada' among Formative-period sites of the Totorilla valley near Huamanga, and the Wari city of Conchopata lies between the Totorilla ravine and the airport runway next to the cuartel (Ochatoma and Cabrera 2000), but no source links the Formative 'La Hoyada' to the cuartel's La Hoyada sector or places Wari/Huarpa remains inside the perimeter
