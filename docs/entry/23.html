Nevada National Security Site (Nevada Test Site / Nevada Proving Ground)

Nevada, US · US contiguous West/Southwest · AEC/DOE 1951 (carved from the USAAF gunnery range) · founded 1951 (1951–1992 testing; active DOE site)

Evidence: Confirmed Overlaps documented sites

What lies beneath

Paiute and Shoshone occupation >10,000 years; rock shelters, petroglyphs, Tippipah Spring, Wunjiakuda rock; 1996 NAGPRA consultation on removed artifacts; Western Shoshone Ruby Valley treaty claim

Relationship

Overlaps documented sites
