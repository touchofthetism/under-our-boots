Tumaco — Base Naval del Pacífico

Nariño, Colombia · South America · Colombian Navy · founded 1950 (20th c.; active)

Evidence: Probable Overlaps documented sites

What lies beneath

The El Morro site, on Isla El Morro at the entrance to Tumaco bay where the naval complex stands: a 1996 excavation by J.-F. Bouchard (CNRS) 'en los terrenos del Centro de Control de Contaminacion del Pacifico' (CCCP, a DIMAR/Armada Nacional facility) found a Tumaco-La Tolita occupation followed by a 'Morro' phase midden dated c. 350-450 CE; 1978 trenches were dug in the Capitania de Puerto. Bouchard notes no pre-Hispanic remains have been found on the other Tumaco islands, so the earlier 'island-wide' reading is narrowed to El Morro
