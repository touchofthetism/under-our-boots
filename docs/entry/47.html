Camp Ripley (incl. Fort Ripley site)

Minnesota, US · US Plains/Midwest/South-Central · Minnesota National Guard 1930 (US Army Fort Ripley 1849–77) · founded 1849 (1849–1877; 1930–present; active)

What lies beneath

Ojibwe and Ho-Chunk territory; Fort Ripley ruins plus recorded pre-contact sites inside the training area, including Woodland campsite 21MO24 (Middle/Late Woodland, Havana, Sandy Lake ceramics; Phase II 1993) and sites 21MO27 and 21MO32 (Phase II), from Army Corps and MN National Guard-sponsored surveys (Birk 1987 and later)
