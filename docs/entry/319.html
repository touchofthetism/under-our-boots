Prisión Militar / Cuartel de Santiago Tlatelolco

Mexico City, Mexico · Mexico and Central America · Spain (Franciscan convent 16th c.); Mexican Army (military prison and barracks under Porfirio Díaz) · founded 1876 (c. 1876–1945/48; now Zona Arqueológica de Tlatelolco)

Evidence: Confirmed Built directly on a known site

What lies beneath

Tlatelolco ceremonial centre (Late Postclassic with Teotihuacan and Preclassic occupation): Templo Mayor of Tlatelolco, Templo Calendárico, tzompantli, Gran Basamento, Tecpan

Relationship

Built directly on a known site

Flags

Restricted access; Destroyed

Restricted access/Destroyed (the military prison and railway warehouse occupied the precinct until 1945–48; systematic excavation only began in 1944 'among the prison walls'; the 1960s housing project then threatened the ruins — 67 structures saved; Tecpan partially demolished 1964 — civil works)
