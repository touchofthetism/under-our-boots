Castillo de Chapultepec / Colegio Militar

Mexico City, Mexico · Mexico and Central America · Spain (viceregal residence 1785); Mexican Army (barracks 1806; Colegio Militar 1841–1914) · founded 1806 (1806–1914; INAH museum)

Evidence: Probable Built directly on a known site

What lies beneath

Mexica sacred hill: a summit teocalli (destroyed at the conquest); rock reliefs of Mexica rulers on the eastern slope (only Moctezuma II's survives); a pre-Hispanic temple to Ehécatl; Teotihuacan-period remains found 2000; pre-Hispanic water container and spring at the foot

Relationship

Built directly on a known site

also deliberate reuse of a sacred place

Flags

Destroyed

Destroyed (Bishop Zumárraga ordered the ruler reliefs destroyed; the 1785 access road destroyed the San Miguel chapel and several carved busts; 1916 demolition of most of the Colegio Militar); INAH's 2023 bulletin corrected the Baños de Moctezuma myth (the ponds are 19th-century)
