Fort Campbell

Kentucky/Tennessee, US · US East/Southeast · US Army · founded 1942 (1942–present; active (102,414 acres))

Evidence: Confirmed Overlaps documented sites

What lies beneath

Army 1997 NAGPRA report: minimum 442 sites; the U.S. Army Garrison Fort Campbell published a NAGPRA Notice of Inventory Completion in the Federal Register (24 April 2019) for Native American human remains and associated funerary objects removed from Fort Campbell, found culturally unidentifiable and offered for transfer to the consulting tribes
