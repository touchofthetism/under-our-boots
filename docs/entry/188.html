Marine Corps Base Hawaii, Kāneʻohe Bay (Mōkapu Peninsula; Fort Hase; NAS Kaneohe Bay)

Hawaii, US · Hawaii and Alaska · US Army 1918; US Navy 1939–41; USMC 1952 · founded 1918 (1918–present; active (entire 2,951-acre peninsula))

Evidence: Confirmed Built directly on a known site

What lies beneath

Mōkapu, Heleloa and Heʻeia dune burial grounds — the largest documented pre-contact Native Hawaiian burial population: minimum 1,582 individuals removed 1915–93 (799 from Heʻeia 1938–40; 108 found in 1975 base construction); Ulupaʻu Crater; Nuʻupia fishponds; new iwi kūpuna still found (one in Jan 2024)

Destroyed; Land seizure / displacement; Repatriation / NAGPRA

Destroyed (excavations and construction); Land seizure (1940 Navy condemnation of 464 acres); Repatriation (FR 22 Apr 1998 inventory; April 1999 repatriation of 'the largest collection of pre-contact Native Hawaiian burials'; OHA: the count nearly doubled on examination; reburial still unfinished; FR 2025-04364 of 18 Mar 2025, Notice of Intended Disposition for ≥57 individuals recovered 1999–2023; FR 2025-04462 of 19 Mar 2025 for one individual encountered 24 Jan 2024)
