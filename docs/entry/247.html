What lies beneath

Cabrits/Fort Shirley (Dominica): Honychurch (World Archaeology 28, 1997) reports pre-Columbian stone implements located on the Prince Rupert Bay headland at a place disturbed by the 18th-century British fortifications, opposite the Suazoid-period Carib village site at the Indian River estuary; no pre-contact find is published for Brimstone Hill, Fort Saint-Louis, Fort George, Fort Charlotte, Garrison Savannah, Fort Oranje, Fort Amsterdam or Fort Bay
