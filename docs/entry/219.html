Vieques Naval Training Range (Camp García; Live Impact Area) and Naval Ammunition Support Detachment

Vieques, Puerto Rico (US) · Caribbean and Panama · US Navy · founded 1941 (1941–2003; closed; USFWS refuge / Superfund)

Evidence: Confirmed Overlaps documented sites

What lies beneath

~345 recorded sites on Vieques, of which ~327 lie in the two former Navy sectors; preceramic to Agroalfarero III (Hombre de Puerto Ferro burial c. 1900 BCE; La Hueca culture defined on the island); ≥16 NRHP archaeological listings on Navy land (Algodones, Camp García petroglyphs, Laguna Jalova district, Playa Vieja, Ventana district)

Relationship

Overlaps documented sites

petroglyph and village sites retained inside maneuver and impact areas

Flags

Land seizure / displacement; Destroyed; Restricted access; Litigation / inquiry

Land seizure/displacement (~22,000 acres, two-thirds of the island, 1941–50; relocations some uncompensated — 20th-century residents, not Indigenous); Destroyed (Probable: bombing ~180 days/yr; 23,000 bombs in 1998; no count of sites damaged); Restricted access (965-acre Live Impact Area closed; UXO units closed); Litigation (David Sanes killed 19 Apr 1999; 2001 federal suit; Navy left 1 May 2003)
