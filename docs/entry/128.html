What lies beneath

USAG West Point ICRMP 2011-2016 records 170+ known archaeological sites on the reservation reflecting occupation from the prehistoric to historic era, including a Native American rockshelter in Training Area C that was looted in August 2003; all sites are treated as NRHP-eligible pending SHPO concurrence, with consultation under a 2005 MOU with the Stockbridge-Munsee Band of Mohican Indians
