Camp Ipperwash

Ontario, Canada · Canada and Greenland · Canada (DND) · founded 1942 (1942–c. 1995; returned (settlement 14 Apr 2016); ordnance cleanup ongoing)

Evidence: Confirmed Built directly on a known site

What lies beneath

Stoney Point (Aazhoodena) Anishinaabe reserve community (18 families removed May 1942) and its burial ground (flagged by Chief and Council in 1937; Oct 2010 excavation reported remains ~1,000 years old)

Relationship

Built directly on a known site

camp is the expropriated reserve; burial ground in the adjoining park parcel taken from the same reserve

Flags

Land seizure / displacement; Litigation / inquiry; Destroyed; Restricted access; Concealment (documented)

Land seizure (War Measures Act 1942 after the band refused $15/acre; return promised 1981); Litigation/inquiry (1993 reoccupation; Dudley George shot 6 Sept 1995; OPP officer convicted 1997; Ipperwash Inquiry 2004–07, 100 recommendations; CA$95M settlement 2016); Destroyed (UXO; cleanup up to 20 years); Restricted access; Concealment (documented): the province agreed in 1937 to fence the burial ground, nothing was done and the records were later lost from provincial files
