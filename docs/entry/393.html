Escuela Militar de Montaña 'Juan Domingo Perón' (Ejército Argentino training lands, Bariloche) — Lof Millalonco Ranquehue claim

San Carlos de Bariloche, Río Negro, Argentina · South America · Argentine Army; lands held by the State/Army since 1937 · founded 1937 (1937–present)

Evidence: Probable Territory taken, no located site

What lies beneath

Mapuche ancestral territory occupied by the Millalonco Ranquehue families since the late 19th century, 'desde antes de la instalación de las tropas militares'; INAI survey recognised in 2012; no excavated pre-contact site is cited in the litigation

Relationship

Territory taken, no located site

Mapuche territorial claim over Army training ground; occupation documented from the 1880s, not an excavated pre-Columbian site

Flags

Land seizure / displacement; Restricted access; Litigation / inquiry

Documented litigation: Feb 2022 federal judge ordered the Army to hand the community title within 60 days; Ministry of Defence appealed (late), Cámara Federal de General Roca rejected it; prosecutor Stornelli obtained a 'no innovar' measure (Nov 2022); Corte Suprema (30 Mar 2023) admitted the Army/prosecutor appeals and froze the transfer. Defence argues the land is essential for the Escuela Militar de Montaña's training. Update: Corte Suprema (c. 27 Jul 2024) annulled the 2022 first-instance order and sent the case back for a new trial because Río Negro province had not been given intervention; the community (c. 180 ha claimed within >3,000 ha the Army administers, which the Army says it uses for mule grazing) remains on the land.
