Pacific Missile Range Facility, Barking Sands (Nohili / Mānā)

Hawaii, US · Hawaii and Alaska · US Army (WWII airfield); US Navy; State leases 1964–65 · founded 1940 (1940s–present; active; leases expire 2027–2030)

Evidence: Confirmed Built directly on a known site

What lies beneath

Mānā Plain coastal dunes, 'the burial grounds of ancient Hawaiians' at Nohili; heiau Polihale, ʻElekuna, Kahelu, Hoʻoneʻenuʻu; 19 sites in the leasehold (Nohili Dune eligible); earliest site 11th century; NAGPRA 1999: ≥4 individuals removed 1966–70
