Loma de Tiscapa (Campo de Marte; Guardia Nacional HQ; El Búnker; Hospital Militar)

Managua, Nicaragua · Mexico and Central America · Nicaraguan state 1894; Guardia Nacional; Nicaraguan Army · founded 1894 (1894–present (partial))

Evidence: Unverified Adjacent only

What lies beneath

Crater hill; the large Postclassic (c. 800-1520 CE) Villa Tiscapa site with Sacasa Estriado urn burials, excavated by Lange et al. (1996) and by a 2010s rescue on ENATREL land in the Villa Tiscapa barrio, lies south of the lagoon outside the military/presidential complex; Guido Martinez (Alcaldia de Managua 2021) describes a 'Tiscapa' culture settled south of the Laguna de Tiscapa. No pre-Hispanic find on the Loma itself is published (Acahualinca footprints are in a different barrio)
