Base Aérea de Florianópolis (BAFL) — sítio arqueológico Base Aérea I

Carianos/Tapera, south of Ilha de Santa Catarina, Florianópolis, Santa Catarina, Brazil · South America · Brazilian Air Force (Força Aérea Brasileira), 20th century · founded — (20th century–present)

Evidence: Probable Overlaps documented sites

What lies beneath

Sítio Base Aérea I, a late pre-colonial shallow burial site ('sítio raso de sepultamento') with Itararé-tradition ceramics on a recent marine terrace 'em terrenos da Base Aérea de Florianópolis', dated c. 800 ± 70 BP; excavated by Pe. João Alfredo Rohr in 1958, when only c. 10% of the deposit remained after sand quarrying; skeletal series used in violence/trauma studies (Lessa & Scherer 2008)

Destroyed

Documented: by 1958 the bulk of the site had been destroyed by sand extraction ('exploração de areal'); Rohr also recorded that the old road to the Base Aérea was paved with shells taken from nearby sambaquis (oral information, 1950s–60s). Not attributed to deliberate military action. IPHAN process 1129-T-84 press clipping: workers extracting sand on Base Aérea land unearthed prehistoric skulls and burials under a shell layer before Rohr's excavations.
