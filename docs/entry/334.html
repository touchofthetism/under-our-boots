Castillo de San Felipe de Lara, Río Dulce

Guatemala · Mexico and Central America · Spain 1651 · founded 1651 (1651–1817; monument)

Evidence: Confirmed Built directly on a known site

What lies beneath

Built on part of the San Felipe archaeological site (Voorhies 1969, Yale PhD 'San Felipe: A Prehistoric Settlement in Eastern Guatemala'), a Maya settlement at the Rio Dulce-Lake Izabal narrows occupied continuously from the Middle Preclassic to Spanish contact; 2000 excavations on the castle's foundations recovered Preclassic (Ixtamuy, Jucub), Early/Late Classic and Postclassic sherds mixed with colonial material, and the excavators suggest pre-Hispanic mounds were destroyed to build the platform on which the castle stands (Putzeys and Ortega 2001)
