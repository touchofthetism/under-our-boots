Resolute Bay (Joint Arctic Weather Station / RCAF Station Resolute Bay)

Nunavut, Canada · Canada and Greenland · US and Canada 1947; RCAF 1949 · founded 1949 (1949–1964; closed (civil airport))

Evidence: Confirmed Adjacent only

What lies beneath

Thule-culture ruins (Qarnartakuj) near the hamlet; the 1953 settlement lay 8 km from the base; Pre-Dorset/Dorset on Cornwallis Island; no site under the base footprint

Relationship

Adjacent only

Flags

Land seizure / displacement; Restricted access; Litigation / inquiry

Land seizure/displacement (1953 High Arctic relocation of 92 Inuit to Resolute and Grise Fiord; moves not voluntary per the Qikiqtani Truth Commission); Restricted access (base off-limits to Inuit unless employed; RCAF/DND refused housing, food or medical care); Litigation/inquiry (RCAP 1994; $10M trust 1996; federal apology 18 Aug 2010)
