Kalaeloa (former Naval Air Station Barbers Point; Honouliuli)

Hawaii, US · Hawaii and Alaska · US Navy · founded 1942 (1942–1999; closed (Navy retains 1,055 acres))

Evidence: Confirmed Built directly on a known site

What lies beneath

ʻEwa Plain sinkhole dwellings, agricultural and religious features, probable burials; 77-acre Kalaeloa Heritage Park with >177 features incl. a heiau; NAGPRA 1999: ≥5 individuals and ~356 funerary objects removed 1962–90; Ordy Pond ~7,500-year sediment record with human arrival c. AD 940–1130

Relationship

Built directly on a known site

sinkhole/habitation landscape; overlaps burials

Flags

Destroyed; Repatriation / NAGPRA; Concealment (alleged)

Destroyed (Navy used Ordy Pond as an ordnance dump in the 1960s–70s; ~$1.35M to clear); Repatriation (1999 notice); Concealment allegation (weak: Civil Beat 2025 describes 'cavalier treatment'); HCDA rejected three Navy parcels June 2024 over contamination and archaeological liability
