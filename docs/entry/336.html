British Army Training Support Unit Belize (BATSUB), Price Barracks and jungle training estate

Belize · Mexico and Central America · Britain (British Forces Belize to 1994; BATSUB 1994) · founded 1948 (1948–present; active (treaty renewed 2018))

Evidence: Confirmed Overlaps documented sites

What lies beneath

Training estate of nearly 380,000 ha (about one-sixth of Belize): the MOD's own environmental audit says all training areas are rich in archaeology including significant Maya ruins; Aug 2000 reconnaissance of a proposed mortar range for damage to Maya cave and surface sites; DIO 2024: Maya remnants on the estate, trackers record and report finds

Relationship

Overlaps documented sites

Price Barracks itself not on a documented site

Flags

Land seizure / displacement; Restricted access; Concealment (documented)

Land use without payment (treaty silent; minimal consultation); Restricted access (sensitive areas placed out of bounds); Concealment-adjacent (documented): the audit rates the direct adverse effect of live firing on Maya monuments 'major and long-term' (reported by Declassified UK, 4 Feb 2020)
