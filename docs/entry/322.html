Palacio Nacional (Palacio Virreinal; Primera Zona Militar HQ; SEDENA east wing)

Mexico City, Mexico · Mexico and Central America · Spain (Cortés 1522; Crown 1563); Mexican Army (military-zone HQ 19th c.–present) · founded 1522 (1522–present; active (seat of the executive; World Heritage))

What lies beneath

The Casas Nuevas of Moctezuma Xocoyotzin (1502), five palaces whose stones were reused; INAH rescue work in 1971 found pavement, steps, a room and a probable adoratorio; 2008 excavations found the Casa Denegrida
