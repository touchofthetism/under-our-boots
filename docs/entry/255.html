CFB Suffield (incl. British Army Training Unit Suffield and Suffield National Wildlife Area)

Alberta, Canada · Canada and Greenland · Canada (DND) 1941; British Army from 1971 · founded 1941 (1941–present; active (c. 2,700 km²))

Evidence: Confirmed Overlaps documented sites

What lies beneath

Niitsitapi (Blackfoot) stone features: 1985 survey of only 206 sq mi recorded 3,712 features (2,486 tipi rings, 1,071 cairns, 4 medicine wheels, 5 effigies, a bison kill); two National Historic Sites inside the base (Suffield Tipi Rings; British Block Cairn with a probable central burial pit), designated 15 Nov 1973

Relationship

Overlaps documented sites

densest recorded Plains stone-feature concentration in Alberta

Flags

Restricted access; Destroyed

Restricted access (public access 'strictly prohibited' under the National Defence Act); Destroyed (partial: some cairns excavated, others vandalized; ~1,150 gas wells and a 2005 proposal for 1,275 more inside the NWA)
