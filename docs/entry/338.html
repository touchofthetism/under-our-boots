Fortaleza de San Fernando de Omoa

Honduras · Mexico and Central America · Spain 1759–1775/79; Honduran Army · founded 1775 (1775–late 19th c.; museum)

Evidence: Probable Deliberate reuse of an Indigenous place

What lies beneath

Omoa was an Indigenous town in 1536 (encomienda), entirely depopulated by the late 16th century; no pre-Hispanic structure under the fort documented

Relationship

Deliberate reuse of an Indigenous place

fort at a depopulated Indigenous town site

Flags

Land seizure / displacement

Land seizure/displacement (16th-c. encomienda and depopulation); forced Indigenous and enslaved African labour; prison deaths to 1870
