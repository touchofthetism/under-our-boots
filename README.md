# Under Our Boots — the dataset

**404 military installations built on Indigenous and significant historical sites**,
mapped and graded. This repository is the public dataset and website behind
**Under Our Boots**, Shefton Berthold's TikTok and YouTube Shorts series:
*the history buried beneath America's military bases.*

- **Browse the map and every entry:** the `docs/` site in this repo (GitHub Pages).
- **The data:** [`data/installations.csv`](data/installations.csv) ·
  [`data/installations.geojson`](data/installations.geojson)
- **Episodes:** [`EPISODES.md`](EPISODES.md) — each entry page is the
  "Full entry on the website" linked from that episode's description.
- **How the research team works:** [`TEAM.md`](TEAM.md)

## Evidence levels — facts are stated at their level, never above it

| Level | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Confirmed** | Supported by primary or authoritative sources; stated as fact. |
| **Probable** | Strongly indicated; always attributed in scripts. |
| **Unverified** | Claimed somewhere, not yet verified; never presented as fact. |
| **Refuted** | Checked and contradicted; used only as a myth-buster. |

## Rules that never bend

- **Installation locations only.** Map points are the public locations of
  military installations. Archaeological site and burial locations are never
  published — here or anywhere in the series.
- **No Native Land Digital data** (its terms forbid monetized use).
- Every nation's land is described in that nation's own name, with its
  sovereignty and self-determination recognised.
- Sources for every entry are listed on its page, and every source URL is
  archived in the Wayback Machine (`data/source_snapshots.csv`) so a dead
  link can never erase the evidence.
